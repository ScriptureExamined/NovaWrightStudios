Project Echo Is Almost Here

A Puzzle-Adventure About the Weight of Time

What started as an experiment has become something we’re genuinely proud of. Project Echo — our puzzle-adventure game built around the idea that time doesn’t always move in one direction — is finished and heading into playtesting.

In Echo, the world has slipped. Time folds back on itself, your past actions replay in the environment around you, and the only way forward is to understand what you’ve already done. It’s a game about consequences, observation, and the strange loops that form when cause and effect stop being so cooperative.

If you could rewind a mistake, would you still be the one who made it?

We’re in the final polish pass now and will be submitting for playtesting shortly. Stay tuned — more details, screenshots, and ways to get involved are coming soon.

Stay curious.
– The Dev Team
