# Privacy Policy — NovaWright Firewall Monitor

**Effective Date: September 3, 2026**

NovaWright Studios respects your privacy. **NovaWright Firewall Monitor** is designed to operate locally on your Windows computer and does not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

## 1. Information We Collect

NovaWright Firewall Monitor does **not** collect personal information.

The application does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, telephone number, location, or other identifying information.

The application does not collect or transmit information about your computer, network, browsing activity, or firewall configuration to NovaWright Studios or any third party.

## 2. Firewall Information

NovaWright Firewall Monitor accesses Windows Firewall settings on your computer in order to provide its core functionality.

This may include reading the current status of Windows Firewall profiles and, when requested by the user, temporarily changing the enabled or disabled state of those profiles.

This information is processed locally on your computer and is not transmitted to NovaWright Studios.

## 3. Data Storage

NovaWright Firewall Monitor does not maintain a cloud account or remote database and does not store personal information on NovaWright Studios servers.

Any application settings or preferences used by the application are stored locally on the user's computer as necessary for the application's operation.

## 4. Internet and Network Communications

NovaWright Firewall Monitor does not use internet-based analytics, advertising services, or tracking services.

The application does not transmit personal information or firewall configuration information to NovaWright Studios or other third parties.

## 5. Cookies and Tracking

NovaWright Firewall Monitor does not use cookies.

The application does not use Google Analytics or other analytics or tracking technologies.

The application does not contain advertising or third-party advertising trackers.

## 6. Third-Party Services

NovaWright Firewall Monitor does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services.

The application relies on Windows operating system functionality and APIs to perform its firewall monitoring and management functions.

## 7. Sharing of Information

Because NovaWright Firewall Monitor does not collect personal information, there is no personal information to sell, rent, or share with third parties.

NovaWright Studios will not sell or share information obtained through the application for advertising or marketing purposes.

## 8. Security

Because the application does not transmit personal information to NovaWright Studios or a third-party service, there is no centralized collection of user information associated with the application.

The application uses Windows operating system functionality to access and manage firewall settings. Users should keep Windows and NovaWright Firewall Monitor up to date and use appropriate security practices on their computers.

## 9. Children's Privacy

NovaWright Firewall Monitor does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Because the application does not collect personal information, no special account or registration information is required.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the functionality of NovaWright Firewall Monitor changes in a way that affects privacy or data collection, this Privacy Policy will be updated to accurately describe those changes.

The effective date at the top of this policy will be updated when material changes are made.

## 11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of NovaWright Firewall Monitor, you may contact NovaWright Studios through the contact information provided on the NovaWright Studios website.
