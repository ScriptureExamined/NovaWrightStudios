Introducing Number Push

Number Push is a fresh take on the classic Sokoban-style puzzle game, combining strategic crate pushing with a unique twist. Each crate moves a specific number of spaces per push, challenging players to think ahead, plan carefully, and solve increasingly complex puzzles.

Developed by NovaWright Studios, Number Push offers a rewarding puzzle experience for players who enjoy strategy, logic, and a good mental challenge.
