Dungeon Quest In Development

RPG Adventure Game — PC

Dungeon Quest is a classic-style RPG adventure from NovaWright Studios. Descend into procedurally generated dungeons, battle through monsters and traps, and collect weapons, armor, and loot to grow your character with each run.

Inspired by the dungeon crawlers of earlier eras, Dungeon Quest focuses on straightforward fun: explore, fight, upgrade, and go deeper. Every run generates a different dungeon layout, keeping each playthrough fresh.

Planned Features

  • Procedurally generated dungeon layouts with hand-crafted rooms
  • Turn-based combat with a variety of enemy types
  • Weapon, armor, and item progression across runs
  • Multiple character classes with different playstyles
  • Boss encounters at the end of each dungeon tier
  • Persistent unlocks that carry between runs

Development Status

Dungeon Quest is currently in development at NovaWright Studios. Core mechanics are being built and refined. Release details will be announced here and on the site's news feed as development progresses.
