Temporal Tinkerer Walkthrough

Every step of the mystery, in order, from the letter in the mailbox to the last date on the device.

Spoilers ahead. This walkthrough gives away every clue and puzzle solution. Temporal Tinkerer is still in development, so each step is tagged: In the game works now, Partly in the game some of it works, and Coming soon is designed but not in the game yet. The coloured era label shows when the step takes place. Dates you learn go into your journal and the device's Known Dates list.

Part 1: The Letter

Where the game begins, at the player's own house.

  1. Take the letter from the mailbox 2026 In the game

    A mail truck delivers Tobias's letter and drives off. Click the mailbox, click its door to open it, then click the letter. It fades into the journal and the trunk key goes into your bag. Read the letter in the journal (it names Agnes Mae Whitaker in MillHaven), close the journal, then click Head up to the house.

  2. Get into Tobias's house 2026 In the game

    At the front, click the house. In 2026 the door is standing open (the house has been ransacked), so click it to walk straight into the foyer. The locked door and the welcome-mat key only exist on Nov 7, 2026 (and in 1947), when the house is shut: click the door (locked), click the welcome mat and peel it up, take the key, select it in your bag and use it on the door. The door then stays unlocked in every era.

Part 2: The Ransacked House

Tobias is missing and the house has been searched. Everything here is 2026, the starting era.

  1. Look at the foyer clock 2026 In the game

    The grandfather clock in the foyer has stopped at 11:47. It is a quiet hint at the year 1947.

  2. Take the two photographs from the living room 2026 In the game

    Go through the door on the left of the hallway. Click each photo on the mantel; closing the close-up puts it in your bag. One shows Eleanor and Tobias at the Harwick Fair (1958), the other Tobias with you. You need both later.

  3. Find the trail note in the study 2026 In the game

    The study is at the far end of the hallway. Turn with the left and right arrows to the view behind the desk (facing the door) and open the top-right drawer. Read the note and close it; it goes into the journal: “R.A. never wasted breath. The signs are mostly wind, meant to tire out trespassers. Only the third counts, and only its first. Count in threes.” The wall safe is open and empty, the desk calendar is too damaged to read and the corkboard clues cannot be examined. All of these matter two days earlier.

Part 3: The Forest Signs

Two paths leave the side yard. Each is a short maze of forks, and each sign hides its own route.

  1. Read both signs 2026 In the game

    At the forest entrance click each sign. The note says to take every third word and use only its first letter. Left sign (Ashworth Hollow, est. 1893): long, roads, lead, right, long, roads, lead, left, right gives L R L R L R L L R. Right sign (MillHaven, est. 1887): rough, lanes, run, low, rough, left, rise, rarely, level gives R L R L R L R R L.

  2. Walk the forest 2026 In the game

    Forest scenes alternate between a straight stretch (click forward) and a fork (click left or right). Take the route from the sign at each fork. A wrong turn sends you wandering through extra scenes until the woods bring you back. Once a path is solved it becomes fast travel, and you skip the maze from then on. Do the right-hand path first: the letter sends you to Agnes in MillHaven.

Part 4: MillHaven: The Time Device

Agnes Mae Whitaker is keeping something of Tobias's. She wants proof that you are who you say you are.

  1. Walk into town 2026 In the game

    Click the distant town, then the town ahead and the street ahead (the cursor changes to a walking arrow), through the three approach pictures. The fourth picture is the town hub. Its six buildings are Agnes's house, the Historical Society, the empty storefront, County Records, the diner and the church.

  2. Earn Agnes's trust 2026 In the game

    Click Agnes's house to reach her closed door, then click the door to knock. Agnes appears at the door and speaks. Talk to her and choose “My Uncle Tobias told me to come see you.” She asks for proof. Show her Tobias's letter; she recognises his handwriting and asks for the key. Show her the trunk key. She believes you. Click Leave and the porch is empty with the door standing open: click the doorway to go in (her front room, the stairs to the attic). If you leave before she trusts you, she shuts the door and you must knock again.

  3. Open the trunk in the attic 2026 In the game

    Ask about what Tobias left; she tells you it is in the attic trunk. Go in, take the stairs up, then select the trunk key and use it on the lock. Take the brass time device and the sealed letter from Tobias. The letter goes into the journal and explains the device: turn the month and day with the arrows, type the year, press Travel. The Known Dates list fills in the dials for you. Close enough counts for most dates (within about a week), but Nov 7, 2026 must be exact.

Part 5: Two Days Ago

The house on Nov 7, 2026, before it was ransacked. Everything the searchers destroyed is still here.

  1. Learn the date Nov 7, 2026 2026 In the game

    Reading Tobias's letter from the trunk (page two) says: “I'll be at my desk until the seventh of November, with everything laid out for anyone who comes looking.” Reading it adds Nov 7, 2026 to the device's Known Dates as “The last day Tobias was at his desk”. The letter never says to dial it: the player works out that this is a date to travel to.

  2. Travel to Nov 7, 2026 Two days earlier In the game

    At the house, open the device, enter Nov 7, 2026 (this date must be exact) and press Travel. The house is intact and warm, and Tobias is already gone.

  3. Read the desk calendar Two days earlier In the game

    In the study, click the desk surface. In the close-up, click the calendar: March 15 is circled, and the year showing is 1947. It goes into the journal with that date, so March 15, 1947 joins the device's Known Dates.

  4. Read the corkboard Two days earlier In the game

    Click the corkboard, then click each clue: a note reading “R.A. - confirm before the 15th” and Tobias's timeline (1939 estate in family, 1942 new plans, 1947 circled, 1956 transfer records, 1962 house vacant). Both go into the journal.

  5. Open the safe Two days earlier In the game

    Turn to the wall with the painting and click the painting to swing it open. The wall safe has a keypad. Type 31547 (3-15-47, from the calendar and the note), then click the handle. Take the deed photograph and the workshop key. Items you carry stay with you when you travel.

  6. Return home 2026 In the game

    Open the device, choose Home, today (Nov 9, 2026) and travel back. Agnes asks for the deed photograph once you have found out who C. Vance is (see “Show Agnes the deed photograph” in Part 7).

Part 6: Ashworth Hollow: The Fire Date

The left-hand forest path comes out at the end of a single street. Walk up it in three stops, and click a building to turn to it. Back returns to the street.

  1. Walk up the street and read the Gazette 2026 In the game

    Solve the left-hand path (the signs above). At the first stop turn left to the burned workshop. Click the newspaper box on its post at the left of the picture. The box opens in a close-up. Click the stack of papers to read the front page of the Millhaven Gazette, Friday, June 15, 1962: Workshop at Ashworth Hollow destroyed by fire. It goes into the journal and adds June 14, 1962 (the night of the fire) to the Known Dates. This is the only place the fire date comes from. It does not give June 15; that comes later from Agnes.

  2. Look around the Hollow 2026 In the game

    At the first stop the notice board holds only the ghost of faded writing. Past the cottage and icehouse, the far end has a covered bridge and a round bare patch of earth where something heavy once stood. It reads vague at first; once you know the 1947 boundary stone or the June 13 digging, clicking it says more. The cottage is empty and the icehouse is sealed.

Part 7: MillHaven: The Missing Gazette

June 1962 is missing from the town’s newspaper archive, and the deed’s date looks wrong. Thread A (the Gazette) must be followed all the way to the 1962 library basement: it holds the clue that makes Eleanor talk in Part 8. Thread B (the deed) leads to the original deed.

  1. Thread A: Historical Society 2026 In the game

    Talk to Reginald (ask about the Society, then the archive). He alternates lines with you like every other character: his line, yours, his reply. In the reading room go through to the archive shelves and click the 1962 volume: the pages run from May straight to July, with a stub where June was cut out. Ask Reginald about it: he says Walter Pritchard’s father did the renovations that summer, and Walter is in the old storefront on Main Street.

  2. Thread A: Walter Pritchard and the ledger 2026 In the game

    In the empty storefront, talk to Walter and ask about himself, then about his father’s renovation work. Walter no longer hands over a date. In the back room click the open ledger on the stool: “June 12. Library wing, basement. Moved the bound newspapers down overnight, no questions. Paid double.” Reading it adds June 12, 1962 to the Known Dates and a journal entry, The bound Gazettes, June 12, 1962, which tells you the library wing is now the Historical Society. This is the prompt that sends you to the basement.

  3. Thread A: The library basement, 1962 1962 In the game

    Go to the Historical Society, open the device and enter June 12, 1962. The building is the library wing. Go through the door marked GAZETTE ARCHIVE and down the stairs, click the crate at the back to find the 1962 volume, then click the page with the rectangle cut out of it: the journal entry The Gazette volume, June 1962 records the cut (and Pritchard moving the volumes that night). Then read the last page before the cut, June 11, on the left. A boxed report says Constance Vance and Cornelius Montclair were married on Saturday at the First Church, and that Cornelius has been “a familiar face at the Harwick Diner all week”. It goes into the journal as The wedding report. It adds no date (the wedding day stays secret). Eleanor needs this in Part 8.

  4. Thread B: County Records 2026 In the game

    Talk to Marjorie: ask what is kept, then to look up a property, then about the Ashworth property. She points out Deed Book 14 in the deed room. Go through the gate and click the open page. It is dated 1947 but sits among entries from June 1962. Ask Marjorie about the date: the paper is too new, and the notary was a C. Vance. She suggests the church, and asks you to show her anything of the original.

  5. Thread B: The church 2026 In the game

    Talk to the Reverend and ask for C. Vance. He sends you to the vestry. On the shelf of yearly registers, click the 1962 volume; it opens on the desk. In the close-up: the marriage of Cornelius Montclair and Constance Vance in June 1962, bride aged 21. Talk to the Reverend again: she was a child in 1947, so she cannot have witnessed that deed. The deed page was forged in 1962. The register also lists Agnes as a witness.

  6. Thread B: Agnes, the cousin 2026 In the game

    Tell Agnes “A C. Vance notarized the 1947 deed, and you witnessed Constance Vance’s wedding. Is she your cousin?” Constance is her cousin. She tells how Cornelius had Constance sign papers the week before the wedding, and finishes by asking to see what Tobias locked away. That puts Look in my bag on her conversation.

  7. Show Agnes the deed photograph 2026 In the game

    Close the conversation with Look in my bag, select the deed photograph (from the Nov 7 safe) and click Agnes. She tells the first half: Rutherford Ashworth deeded the land to the family in 1947, Cornelius wanted the estate to live there with Constance, so the county’s deed was swapped for a forgery in 1962 with Constance’s name as notary, and Tobias went back to prove it. A photograph is not enough; she wants the original.

  8. Show Marjorie the deed photo 2026 In the game

    Back at County Records, select the deed photograph and click Marjorie. She compares it with the typed Book 14 page: the photograph shows the real deed, with the embossed county seal and a recording stamp of March 21, 1947. The vault register says the original was signed out once, on the day it was filed, by a smudged initial, and never returned. The Book 14 page was typed years later to cover for it. This adds March 21, 1947 (the day it was recorded; the deed itself was drawn up on the 15th) to the Known Dates. Her Look in my bag choice goes away once she has confirmed the forgery.

  9. Recover the original deed at County Records, 1947 1947 In the game

    Go to County Records and enter March 21, 1947; the office changes to 1947. Talk to Mr. Pruitt: ask whether the Ashworth deed has been recorded (it is in vault box nine), then select the deed photograph in your bag and click him. Tobias photographed it at this counter and told him someone would bring the photograph back. Pruitt leaves the vault open. Go through the gate to the deed room, click the vault door, then click the envelope in box nine: you now carry the original Ashworth deed (it also goes into the journal), and the deed photograph leaves your bag. Before March 21 Pruitt only says the deed is not filed yet.

  10. Show Agnes the original deed 2026 In the game

    Back in 2026, select the original Ashworth deed and click Agnes. She tells the whole story and ends with a hint: Tobias’s workshop burned in June 1962, and his logbook may still be there. You still need the logbook to learn when to find him.

  11. Optional secret: the wedding 1962 In the game

    Enter June 9, 1962 in the device. It is not in the Known Dates; it is an easter egg. You watch the Montclair and Vance wedding from the back of the packed church. There is nothing to do there; Back returns.

Part 8: 1962: Eleanor and the Fire

The night of June 14, 1962. Eleanor, a young waitress, saw something she never told anyone.

  1. Find out who Eleanor is 2026 In the game

    In the Harwick Diner in 2026, talk to Dolores, then select the photo of Eleanor and Tobias at the fair in your bag and click her. She says it is Eleanor Reid, who ran the diner’s night counter in the sixties and left town in about 1966. Her Look in my bag choice goes away once you have shown it.

  2. Travel to the 1962 diner 1962 In the game

    Standing in the diner, enter any date from June 8 to June 14, 1962 (the June 14 date comes from the Gazette). You arrive in the 1962 diner.

  3. Earn Eleanor’s trust 1962 In the game

    Ask “Are you Eleanor?” She will not talk to strangers. Close the conversation with Look in my bag, select the fair photo, then click Eleanor: she recognises it but wants more. Select the photo of you with Tobias and click her again (it only works after the fair photo). She believes you, and the bag choice disappears.

  4. Hear what she saw 1962 In the game

    Ask whether she was working the night of the fire. On Thursday, June 14, a little before midnight, she saw Cornelius Montclair’s black Cadillac coming off the Hollow road with its headlights off. About an hour later the sky over the trees went orange.

  5. Ask about the week before the fire 1962 In the game

    This topic only appears once you have seen Cornelius at the diner (the Cadillac story) and read the wedding report in the library basement. Ask whether Cornelius said anything that week: the night before he asked what hour the Hollow road goes quiet. She also gives you a hint about the mill. This adds June 13, 1962 to the Known Dates: the day of the digging, the payoff in Part 9.

Part 9: 1962: Ashworth Hollow Before the Fire

The workshop stands whole. Travel from the Hollow itself so you arrive on its street.

  1. Travel to the Hollow in 1962 1962 In the game

    Stand in the Hollow and enter a date from June 8 to June 14, 1962 (must not be June 15, when the fire has happened). The workshop is whole and padlocked, the cottage is lived in, and the notice board has been torn down.

  2. See the dug-up ground (June 13 and 14) 1962 In the game

    Use June 13 or 14, 1962 (Eleanor’s date). Walk to the far end of the street: the ground by the bridge has been dug out, with tyre ruts from the road to the hole (a new picture; before June 13 the far end looks as it always did). Click the dug-up ground. If you have already seen the 1947 stone (the estate notice or young Amos), the journal entry reads The stone is gone: the boundary stone with the A has been dug out. Otherwise it is only A hole by the bridge: something heavy was taken, and you do not yet know what. The facts stone_dug_up and hollow_marker_missing are learned either way. In 2026 the bare patch at the same spot now reads stronger.

  3. Read the caretaker’s note 1962 In the game

    Go up the street to the second stop and turn left to the cottage. Nobody answers the door. Click the flowerpot by the steps to read the note left there: “Tuesday, June 12, 1962. Tobias, I’ve gone to Millhaven for the week’s supplies, back Friday. The package is in the icehouse, where we agreed. The latch is not locked. The little key is inside with the letter. And please be careful. Someone has been asking about the workshop.” It goes into the journal.

  4. Retrieve Tobias’s package from the icehouse 1962 In the game

    Turn right at the second stop and click the icehouse door. Click the parcel on the stone shelf to unwrap it. Inside are a sealed letter and a small brass key; take them in either order. The letter (dated June 12, 1962, unsigned) warns Tobias not to be in the workshop after dark that week and mentions a stranger asking about the Ashworth land. It goes into the journal. The small key opens the cabinet under Tobias’s bench in the workshop.

  5. Unlock the workshop and take the logbook 1962 In the game

    At the first stop, turn left and click the double doors. Select the workshop key (from the safe) in your bag and click the doors: the padlock springs open and the key is used up. Click the doors again to go inside. The workshop has three views (just inside, the workbench, the blackboard wall). At the bench, click the small cabinet and use the small brass key to open it. Take the logbook: it goes into the journal and adds March 21, 1947 and April 3, 1947 (the boundary stone photo) to the Known Dates. It also names the Montclairs and ends the night of the fire.

  6. Show Agnes the logbook 2026 In the game

    Back in 2026, select the logbook and click Agnes (she only asks once she has seen the original deed). The log ends the night of the fire, June 14, 1962, and she realises Tobias never came back from it: his machine failed and he is stuck in June 1962. She tells you to go to June 15, 1962 and begin at the Hollow. This is the only place June 15, 1962 comes from: it is added to the Known Dates when she says it.

Part 10: 1947: The Estate at Its Best

The deed era. The Hollow has its 1947 pictures, with the readable notice, the boundary stone and young Amos.

  1. Travel to the Hollow in 1947 1947 In the game

    Stand in the Hollow and enter March 15, 1947 (from the calendar) or April 3, 1947. The workshop is in daily use, smoke rises from every chimney, and the stone arch of the icehouse is clean.

  2. Read the estate notice 1947 In the game

    At the first stop, turn right to the notice board. Click the notice to read it full screen. It is signed by Rutherford Ashworth and marks the boundary at the covered bridge and a granite stone cut with the letter A. It goes into the journal (fact hollow_boundary_stone_1947).

  3. Find the boundary stone 1947 In the game

    Walk to the far end of the street. The squared granite boundary stone, carved with an A, stands in the grass beside the bridge; in 2026 only a bare patch remains. Clicking it adds a journal entry. Seeing it here is what makes you recognise it in Part 11.

  4. Meet young Amos and take the wren 1947 In the game

    Click the caretaker’s cottage door: young Amos Pike. Ask who he is, about the boundary stone and about Tobias in the workshop. Then ask about his hands: “That’s fine whittling. What are you making?” He gives you a wooden wren, and your journal notes to show it to him if you ever need a friend on this land. This is required: it opens old Amos’s way in on June 15, 1962.

Part 11: The Finale: June 15, 1962

The rescue.

  1. Arrive the morning after the fire 1962 In the game

    Enter June 15, 1962 (from Agnes). At the Hollow the workshop is a smoking ruin (new picture). The tyre tracks in the ash are wide and heavy: a big car came in the night. The newspaper box now holds the Gazette of the fire. Click the caretaker’s cottage door: Amos Pike answers.

  2. Win Amos’s trust with the wren 1962 In the game

    Ask who he is and what happened: a motor in the lane after two, then smoke; he saw Tobias carried out, hands tied, into a black car that went up the road to the big house. He is wary and wants something from the Estate itself, not papers (his answer points to April 3, 1947 in your journal). Close the conversation with Look in my bag, select the wooden wren and click Amos: he remembers a lad fifteen years ago (1947) who looked just like you, and he trusts you. Now ask “How do I get in?”: not by the front, but round the left side, to the barred cellar window below the new Mrs. Montclair’s room. Then select the original deed and knock again: he tells you to take it to her. Without the wren he only hints that you must bring him something from the Estate.

  3. The Ashworth house: the front 1962 In the game

    Walk the forest path to the house; on June 15, 1962 it takes you to the manor, not the usual front. A guard stands in front of the door with arms folded, the Cadillac sits in the drive and crates are stacked on the porch. The lawn at the bottom-left does not lead round the side until Amos has told you the way: before that it only says there is a worn path and that someone nearby may know a way in. Back walks out to the Hollow.

  4. The stone under the hydrangeas 1962 In the game

    At the side of the house, look low in the hydrangeas beneath the cellar window: a grey stone with a large A, fresh earth on it, two wide tyre ruts leading to it (new picture). Click it. If you have seen the 1947 stone, the journal entry The stone at the manor says it is the Hollow boundary stone, dug up and hidden here, and you learn stone_identified. If not, it reads as A stone under the hydrangeas, an unexplained hidden stone. Coming back later with the 1947 knowledge upgrades the entry to The stone is the boundary stone. It is evidence of where the Ashworth land really ends.

  5. Constance at the window 1962 In the game

    Click the young woman at the upstairs window. She is Constance Montclair, a bride of six days (born Constance Vance, a notary’s daughter). Ask who is in the cellar (her husband calls him a thief but hasn’t sent for the sheriff), then ask whether she notarized a deed for him (she did, a week before the wedding, unread, signed “C. Vance”). Then select the original Ashworth deed in your bag and click her: the date and county seal prove the one she stamped was a lie. She will unbolt the cellar and telephone Sheriff Dunn. The barred cellar window now opens when you click it.

  6. Free Tobias 1962 In the game

    Down in the cellar Tobias is tied to a chair (he speaks first). Talk to him: the broken device (cracked dial, bent coil), Cornelius’s plan, and show him the deed and your read of his logbook if you like. Click the ropes to cut him free, then click the device on the floor: he takes it himself. Click the stairs to go up.

  7. Cornelius is arrested, and you go home 1962 In the game

    The last picture: Sheriff Dunn’s deputies lead Cornelius Montclair off in handcuffs while Constance watches from the porch. Tobias turns the cracked dial and says they are going home. The screen fades to black and the game goes home to 2026: Agnes and Tobias on her porch (a warm conversation: Tobias has asked her to marry him, and she is moving into the estate). Leaving it fades to black and then fades in Tobias’s logbook, where a last entry is written out in his handwriting; a click returns to the title screen.
