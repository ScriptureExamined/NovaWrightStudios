Temporal Tinkerer Walkthrough

Every step of the mystery, in order, from the letter in the mailbox to the last date on the device.

Spoilers ahead. This walkthrough gives away every clue and puzzle solution. Temporal Tinkerer is still in development, so each step is tagged: In the game works now, Partly in the game some of it works, and Coming soon is designed but not in the game yet. The coloured era label shows when the step takes place. Dates you learn go into your journal and the device's Known Dates list.

Part 1: The Letter

Where the game begins, at the player's own house.

  1. Take the letter from the mailbox 2026 In the game

    A mail truck delivers Tobias's letter and drives off. Click the mailbox, click its door to open it, then click the letter. It fades into the journal and the trunk key goes into your bag. Read the letter in the journal (it names Agnes Mae Whitaker in MillHaven), close the journal, then click Head up to the house.

  2. Get into Tobias's house 2026 In the game

    At the front door, click the door for a close-up: it is locked. Go back and click the welcome mat and peel it up. A key is underneath; take it. Select that key in your bag and use it on the door.

Part 2: The Ransacked House

Tobias is missing and the house has been searched. Everything here is 2026, the starting era.

  1. Look at the foyer clock 2026 In the game

    The grandfather clock in the foyer has stopped at 11:47. It is a quiet hint at the year 1947.

  2. Take the two photographs from the living room 2026 In the game

    Go through the door on the left of the hallway. Click each photo on the mantel; closing the close-up puts it in your bag. One shows Eleanor and Tobias at the Harwick Fair (1958), the other Tobias with you. You need both later.

  3. Find the trail note in the study 2026 In the game

    The study is at the far end of the hallway. Turn with the left and right arrows to the view behind the desk (facing the door) and open the top-right drawer. Read the note and close it; it goes into the journal: “R.A. never wasted breath. The signs are mostly wind, meant to tire out trespassers. Only the third counts, and only its first. Count in threes.” The wall safe is open and empty, the desk calendar is too damaged to read and the corkboard clues cannot be examined. All of these matter two days earlier.

Part 3: The Forest Signs

Two paths leave the side yard. Each is a short maze of forks, and each sign hides its own route.

  1. Read both signs 2026 In the game

    At the forest entrance click each sign. The note says to take every third word and use only its first letter. Left sign (Ashworth Hollow, est. 1893): long, roads, lead, right, long, roads, lead, left, right gives L R L R L R L L R. Right sign (MillHaven, est. 1887): rough, lanes, run, low, rough, left, rise, rarely, level gives R L R L R L R R L.

  2. Walk the forest 2026 In the game

    Forest scenes alternate between a straight stretch (click forward) and a fork (click left or right). Take the route from the sign at each fork. A wrong turn sends you wandering through extra scenes until the woods bring you back. Once a path is solved it becomes fast travel, and you skip the maze from then on. Do the right-hand path first: the letter sends you to Agnes in MillHaven.

Part 4: MillHaven: The Time Device

Agnes Mae Whitaker is keeping something of Tobias's. She wants proof that you are who you say you are.

  1. Walk into town 2026 In the game

    Click the distant town, then the town ahead and the street ahead (the cursor changes to a walking arrow), through the three approach pictures. The fourth picture is the town hub. Its six buildings are Agnes's house, the Historical Society, the empty storefront, County Records, the diner and the church.

  2. Earn Agnes's trust 2026 In the game

    Click Agnes's house to reach her closed door, then click the door to knock. Agnes appears at the door and speaks. Talk to her and choose “My Uncle Tobias told me to come see you.” She asks for proof. Show her Tobias's letter; she recognises his handwriting and asks for the key. Show her the trunk key. She believes you. Click Leave and the porch is empty with the door standing open: click the doorway to go in (her front room, the stairs to the attic). If you leave before she trusts you, she shuts the door and you must knock again.

  3. Open the trunk in the attic 2026 In the game

    Ask about what Tobias left; she tells you it is in the attic trunk. Go in, take the stairs up, then select the trunk key and use it on the lock. Take the brass time device and the sealed letter from Tobias. The letter goes into the journal and explains the device: turn the month and day with the arrows, type the year, press Travel. The Known Dates list fills in the dials for you. Close enough counts for most dates (within about a week), but Nov 7, 2026 must be exact.

Part 5: Two Days Ago

The house on Nov 7, 2026, before it was ransacked. Everything the searchers destroyed is still here.

  1. Learn the date Nov 7, 2026 2026 In the game

    Reading Tobias's letter from the trunk (page two) says: “I'll be at my desk until the seventh of November, with everything laid out for anyone who comes looking.” Reading it adds Nov 7, 2026 to the device's Known Dates as “The last day Tobias was at his desk”. The letter never says to dial it: the player works out that this is a date to travel to.

  2. Travel to Nov 7, 2026 Two days earlier In the game

    At the house, open the device, enter Nov 7, 2026 (this date must be exact) and press Travel. The house is intact and warm, and Tobias is already gone.

  3. Read the desk calendar Two days earlier In the game

    In the study, click the desk surface. In the close-up, click the calendar: March 15 is circled, and the year showing is 1947. It goes into the journal with that date, so March 15, 1947 joins the device's Known Dates.

  4. Read the corkboard Two days earlier In the game

    Click the corkboard, then click each clue: a note reading “R.A. - confirm before the 15th” and Tobias's timeline (1939 estate in family, 1942 new plans, 1947 circled, 1956 transfer records, 1962 house vacant). Both go into the journal.

  5. Open the safe Two days earlier In the game

    Turn to the wall with the painting and click the painting to swing it open. The wall safe has a keypad. Type 31547 (3-15-47, from the calendar and the note), then click the handle. Take the deed photograph and the workshop key. Items you carry stay with you when you travel.

  6. Return home 2026 In the game

    Open the device, choose Home, today (Nov 9, 2026) and travel back. Tell Agnes about the deed photo: she says Tobias got to the safe in time (her full explanation is still to be written).

Part 6: Ashworth Hollow: The Fire Date

The left-hand forest path comes out at the end of a single street. Walk up it in three stops, and click a building to turn to it. Back returns to the street.

  1. Walk up the street and read the Gazette 2026 In the game

    Solve the left-hand path (the signs above). At the first stop turn left to the burned workshop. Click the newspaper box on its post at the left of the picture. The box opens in a close-up. Click the stack of papers to read the front page of the Millhaven Gazette, Friday, June 15, 1962: Workshop at Ashworth Hollow destroyed by fire. It goes into the journal and adds June 14, 1962 (the night of the fire) to the Known Dates. This is the only place the fire date comes from.

  2. Look around the Hollow (optional) 2026 In the game

    At the first stop the notice board holds only the ghost of faded writing. Past the cottage and icehouse, the far end has a covered bridge and a round bare patch of earth where something heavy once stood (a boundary stone, now missing). The cottage is empty and the icehouse is sealed.

Part 7: MillHaven: The Missing Gazette

June 1962 is missing from the town's newspaper archive, and the deed's date looks wrong. Two threads can be followed in either order.

  1. Thread A: Historical Society 2026 In the game

    Talk to Reginald (ask about the Society, then the archive). In the reading room go through to the archive shelves and click the 1962 volume: the pages run from May straight to July, with a stub where June was cut out. Ask Reginald about it: he says Walter Pritchard's father did the renovations that summer, and Walter is in the old storefront on Main Street. (The Gazette basement has a plaque reading “Renovated 1962. Pritchard & Son, Builders” and empty shelving.)

  2. Thread A: Walter Pritchard 2026 In the game

    In the empty storefront, talk to Walter and ask about himself (“Have you lived here long?”), then about his father's renovation work. His father moved the bound newspapers into the basement overnight in June 1962. This adds June 12, 1962 to the Known Dates. In the back room click the 1962 ledger: “June 12. Library wing, basement. Moved the bound newspapers down overnight, no questions. Paid double.”

  3. Thread B: County Records 2026 In the game

    Talk to Marjorie: ask what is kept, then to look up a property, then about the Ashworth property. She points out Deed Book 14 in the deed room. Go through the gate and click the open page. It is dated 1947 but sits among entries from June 1962. Ask Marjorie about the date: the paper is too new, and the notary was a C. Vance. She suggests the church.

  4. Thread B: The church 2026 In the game

    Talk to the Reverend and ask for C. Vance. He sends you to the vestry. On the shelf of yearly registers, click the 1962 volume; it opens on the desk. In the close-up: the marriage of Cornelius Montclair and Constance Vance in June 1962, bride aged 21. Talk to the Reverend again: she was a child in 1947, so she cannot have witnessed that deed. The deed page was forged in 1962. The register also lists Agnes as a witness.

  5. Thread B: Agnes, the cousin 2026 In the game

    Tell Agnes “I found your name in the 1962 marriage register.” Constance is her cousin. She tells how Cornelius had Constance sign papers the week before the wedding.

  6. Show Marjorie the deed photo 2026 In the game

    Back at County Records, once you have seen the Book 14 page, choose “I have a photograph of the Ashworth deed.” Marjorie compares it with the typed Book 14 page: the photograph shows the real deed, with the embossed county seal and a recording stamp of March 21, 1947. The vault register says the original was kept in box nine and signed out in June 1962, never returned. She suggests asking whoever stood at the counter the day it was filed. This adds March 21, 1947 (the day it was recorded; the deed itself was drawn up on the 15th) to the Known Dates.

  7. Recover the original deed at County Records, 1947 1947 Coming soon

    Planned. Travel to March 21, 1947 (a new key date, still to be added) and go to the County Records office. Show the deed photograph to the 1947 clerk, who recognises it and hands over the real original (or a certified copy). This needs a 1947 County Records scene and a clerk.

  8. Optional secret: the wedding 1962 In the game

    Enter June 9, 1962 in the device. It is not in the Known Dates; it is an easter egg. You watch the Montclair and Vance wedding from the back of the packed church. There is nothing to do there; Back returns.

Part 8: 1962: Eleanor and the Fire

The night of June 14, 1962. Eleanor, a young waitress, saw something she never told anyone.

  1. Find out who Eleanor is 2026 In the game

    In the Harwick Diner in 2026, talk to Dolores and show her the photo of Eleanor and Tobias at the fair. She says it is Eleanor Reid, who ran the diner's night counter in the sixties and left town in about 1966.

  2. Travel to the 1962 diner 1962 In the game

    Standing in the diner, enter any date from June 8 to June 14, 1962 (the June 14 date comes from the Gazette). You arrive in the 1962 diner.

  3. Earn Eleanor's trust 1962 In the game

    Ask “Are you Eleanor?” She will not talk to strangers. Show her the fair photo and the photo of you with Tobias (in either order). She believes you.

  4. Hear what she saw 1962 In the game

    Ask whether she was working the night of the fire. On Thursday, June 14, a little before midnight, she saw Cornelius Montclair's black Cadillac coming off the Hollow road with its headlights off. About an hour later the sky over the trees went orange. Then ask whether Cornelius said anything that week: the night before he asked what hour the Hollow road goes quiet. This adds June 13, 1962 to the Known Dates.

Part 9: 1962: Ashworth Hollow Before the Fire

The workshop stands whole. Travel from the Hollow itself so you arrive on its street.

  1. Travel to the Hollow in 1962 1962 In the game

    Stand in the Hollow and enter a date from June 8 to June 14, 1962 (must not be June 15, when the fire has happened). The workshop is whole and padlocked, the cottage is lived in, and the notice board has been torn down.

  2. Read the caretaker's note 1962 In the game

    Go up the street to the second stop and turn left to the cottage. Nobody answers the door. Click the flowerpot by the steps to read the note left there: “Tuesday, June 12, 1962. Tobias, I've gone to Millhaven for the week's supplies, back Friday. The package is in the icehouse, where we agreed. The latch is not locked. The little key is inside with the letter. And please be careful. Someone has been asking about the workshop.” It goes into the journal.

  3. Unlock the workshop 1962 Partly in the game

    At the first stop, turn left and click the double doors. With the workshop key (from the safe) the padlock springs open. Click the doors again to go inside. The workshop has three views (just inside, the workbench, the blackboard wall). At the bench, click the small cabinet and use the small brass key (from the icehouse) to open it. Take the logbook: it goes into the journal and adds March 21, 1947 (the deed recorded) and April 3, 1947 (the boundary stone photo) to the Known Dates. It also names the Montclairs and ends the night of the fire. The blackboard and calendar are decoration for now.

  4. Return to Agnes with the original deed 2026 Coming soon

    Planned. Once you hold the original deed, Agnes trusts you with the second half of the story: why Tobias kept going back, and where he was lost in 1962. She gives you the date to dial to find him.

  5. Retrieve Tobias's package from the icehouse 1962 In the game

    Turn right at the second stop and click the icehouse door. Click the parcel on the stone shelf to unwrap it. Inside are a sealed letter and a small brass key; take them in either order. The letter (dated June 12, 1962, unsigned, from the friend who left the cottage note) warns Tobias not to be in the workshop after dark that week and mentions a stranger asking about the Ashworth land. It goes into the journal. The small key opens the cabinet under Tobias's bench in the workshop.

  6. Find the full article in the library basement 1962 Coming soon

    The June 12 date from Walter is meant to lead to the 1962 library wing basement, where the missing Gazette volumes were hidden. This 1962 location is not built yet.

Part 10: 1947: The Estate at Its Best

The deed era. The Hollow has its 1947 pictures, with the readable notice and the boundary stone.

  1. Travel to the Hollow in 1947 1947 Partly in the game

    Stand in the Hollow and enter March 15, 1947 (from the calendar) or April 3, 1947. The workshop is in daily use, smoke rises from every chimney, and the stone arch of the icehouse is clean.

  2. Read the estate notice 1947 In the game

    At the first stop, turn right to the notice board. Click the notice to read it full screen. It marks the Ashworth boundary at the covered bridge and a granite stone cut with the letter A. It goes into the journal. (The wording is a placeholder for now.)

  3. Find the boundary stone 1947 Partly in the game

    Walk to the far end of the street. The squared granite boundary stone, carved with an A, stands in the grass beside the bridge; in 2026 only a bare patch remains. Clicking it records the fact. The photograph that becomes the deed evidence is not built yet.

  4. Meet the caretaker 1947 Coming soon

    The caretaker knew Rutherford Ashworth personally and can confirm the boundary. Not built yet. (Plan change: Tobias is lost in 1962, after the fire, not in 1947.)

Part 11: The Finale

Not built yet. This summarises the planned ending.

  1. Win Rutherford's trust and confirm the deed 1947 Coming soon

    Rutherford Ashworth answers the door at the manor but does not let a stranger in. The player must find Tobias first, then return to Rutherford and earn his trust.

  2. Recover the original deed and bring Tobias home 1947 Coming soon

    Retrieve the original deed, work out how to bring Tobias back, and return to 2026 together on the prototype with the deed and the proof, ending the Montclair claim.
