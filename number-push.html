Number Push Coming Soon

Puzzle Game — PC (Steam)

Number Push is a puzzle game built around one satisfying mechanic: push numbered blocks into the right positions. The rules are simple enough to learn in seconds, but the levels will make you think.

Each puzzle gives you a grid of numbered blocks and a target arrangement. Move blocks around the board, combine values, and clear the grid in as few moves as possible. Early levels ease you in; later levels demand careful planning.

Features

  • Clean, focused puzzle gameplay with no clutter
  • Dozens of hand-crafted levels across multiple difficulty tiers
  • Move counter and best-score tracking per puzzle
  • Relaxed mode and timed challenge options
  • Minimal, distraction-free interface
